**Author/Project**: SupeRR-ClutchML Analytics Project

## 1. Executive Summary

Cricket matches are not won by flat-track stat-padding; they are decided in high-leverage moments where every boundary, dot ball, or wicket produces seismic swings in win probability. Traditional cricket statistics (e.g., raw strike rates or economy rates) fail to separate ordinary match conditions from high-stakes crucible situations. 

**SupeRR-ClutchML** is a machine-learning-driven sports analytics framework designed to quantify delivery-level pressure and evaluate player reliability. By deploying a **Hybrid State-Space Win Probability Leverage Model**, establishing a **Dual Benchmark** (measuring both personal performance lift and situational value added), and enforcing **Bayesian shrinkage** and **consistency regularization**, SupeRR-ClutchML delivers a robust, objective ranking of the **Top 10 Batters** and **Top 10 Bowlers** under pressure who are eligible for the Indian Premier League (IPL).

## 2. Mathematical Definition of Pressure

Pressure is modeled as a dynamic, continuous index $PI \in [0, 100]$ computed for every legal delivery prior to its outcome.

### A. Second Innings (Chases): Win Probability Leverage Index (WPLI)
In run chases, pressure is governed by three primary variables:
1. **Required Run Rate Stress ($\text{RRR\_Stress}$)**:
   $$\text{RRR} = \frac{\text{Runs Remaining} \times 6}{\text{Balls Remaining}}$$
   $$\text{Stress}_{\text{RRR}} = \max\left(0, \; (\text{RRR} - 7.5) \times 7.5\right)$$
2. **Resource Depletion Factor ($\text{Stress}_{\text{Wickets}}$)**:
   $$\text{Stress}_{\text{Wickets}} = \max\left(0, \; (6 - \text{Wickets in Hand}) \times 10.0\right) \quad \text{for } \text{Wickets in Hand} \le 5$$
3. **Closeness / Endgame Proximity**:
   $$\text{Closeness} = 30.0 + (36 - \text{Balls Remaining}) \times 0.8 \quad \text{when } \text{Balls Remaining} \le 36 \text{ and Runs Needed} \le 70$$
4. **Leverage Index ($LI$)**:
   Using a calibrated logistic win-probability model $WP(s) = \sigma(\mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{x})$, the leverage index measures the win-probability swing between an boundary (4 runs) and a wicket:
   $$LI(s) = \frac{|WP(s_{\text{boundary}}) - WP(s_{\text{wicket}})|}{\overline{\Delta WP}}$$

The aggregate chase pressure is given by:
$$PI_{\text{chase}} = \min\left(100, \; 15.0 + \text{Stress}_{\text{RRR}} + \text{Stress}_{\text{Wickets}} + \text{Closeness} + 12.0 \cdot LI\right)$$

### B. First Innings (Setting): Collapse & Death Phase Index
Without an explicit target, first-innings pressure is generated by:
1. **Top-Order Collapse Strain**:
   $$\text{Strain}_{\text{collapse}} = \min\left(100, \; \frac{W_{\text{lost}} - \text{Overs}/2}{2.5} \times 80.0\right) \quad (\text{triggered when } W_{\text{lost}} \ge 2 \text{ in Powerplay})$$
2. **Death-Over Margin (Overs 16–20)**:
   Accelerating run scoring against specialized death bowling while avoiding total team dismissals:
   $$\text{Death\_Scale} = 50.0 + (\text{Overs Elapsed} - 15.0) \times 8.0 + 10.0 \cdot \mathbb{I}(W_{\text{lost}} \ge 6)$$

### C. Match Stakes Multipliers & Calibration
$$PI_{\text{final}} = \min\left(100, \; PI \times M_{\text{stakes}}\right)$$
- **Knockout Fixture** (`knock_out == 1`, Finals, Qualifiers, Eliminators): **$1.25\times$**
- **Tier-1 Tournaments** (IPL 2022–2026, Asia Cup): **$1.15\times$**
- **Bilateral T20Is**: **$1.00\times$**

**Threshold**: Deliveries with $PI_{\text{final}} \ge 65.0$ represent the top 30.5% (61,362 deliveries) of the 200,999-ball dataset and are classified as **High Pressure ($H$)**. The remaining 69.5% form the **Baseline ($B$)**.

## 3. Performance Measurement & Rating Logic

Evaluating clutch performance requires separating individual skill from match context. We apply a **Dual Benchmark**:

### A. Batting Rating Engine
1. **Personal Strike Rate Lift**:
   $$\Delta \text{SR} = \text{SR}_{\text{pressure}} - \text{SR}_{\text{baseline}}$$
2. **Situational Value Added (Runs Above Expected - RAE)**:
   Benchmarked against our ML Expectancy Model $\mathbb{E}[\text{runs} \mid \text{state}_b]$:
   $$\text{RAE} = \sum_{b \in H} (\text{runs}_b - \mathbb{E}[\text{runs}_b])$$
3. **High Dismissal Penalty (Survival Weighting)**:
   In high-pressure situations, losing a wicket drastically lowers win probability. Survival is enforced logarithmically:
   $$W_{\text{survival}} = \frac{\ln(\max(1.0, \text{Avg}_{\text{pressure}}) + 5.0)}{\ln(35.0)}$$
4. **Bayesian Sample Shrinkage & Consistency**:
   $$w_{\text{sample}} = \frac{N_b}{N_b + 40}$$
   $$\text{Consistency} = \max\left(0.6, \; \min\left(1.0, \; 1.0 - \frac{\sigma(S_m)}{\mu(S_m) + 50.0}\right)\right)$$
5. **Composite Clutch Batting Score**:
   $$\text{Score}_{\text{Bat}} = \left[ 0.35 \cdot \frac{\text{SR}_p}{1.5} + 0.25 \cdot \Delta \text{SR} + 0.20 \cdot \frac{\text{RAE}}{N_b} \times 100 + 0.20 \cdot (\text{Avg}_p \times 1.5) \right] \times W_{\text{survival}} \times \text{Consistency} \times w_{\text{sample}}$$

### B. Bowling Rating Engine
Balanced equally across **Run Prevention / Choke (50%)** and **Breakthrough Wickets (50%)**:
1. **Run Prevention Component**:
   - Economy Containment: $\max(0, 11.5 - \text{Econ}_p) \times 8.0$
   - Runs Saved Above Expected per Over: $\left(\frac{\text{RSAE}}{N_b} \times 6.0\right) \times 10.0$
   - Dot Ball Percentage: $30\% \times \text{Dot}\%_p$
2. **Breakthrough Wickets Component**:
   - Wicket Strike Rate Efficiency: $\frac{100}{\text{Wicket\_SR}_p} \times 3.5$
   - Wickets Above Expected & Volume: $0.30 \cdot \max(0, 25 - \text{SR}_p) \times 2.0 + 0.20 \cdot \min(20, \text{Wkts}_p \times 0.4)$
3. **Composite Clutch Bowling Score**:
   $$\text{Score}_{\text{Bowl}} = \left[ 0.50 \cdot \text{RunPrevention} + 0.50 \cdot \text{WicketTaking} \right] \times \text{Consistency} \times \left(\frac{N_b}{N_b + 50}\right)$$

## 4. Fair Comparisons & Eligibility Architecture

1. **IPL Eligibility Constraints**:
   In strict accordance with tournament regulations, Pakistani and Bangladeshi players (126 players identified via team mappings) are excluded from the candidate pool. All players in the final rankings have confirmed IPL representation or roster presence.
2. **Sample Size Thresholds**:
   - Batters: Minimum **75 legal deliveries** faced under high pressure.
   - Bowlers: Minimum **100 legal deliveries** bowled under high pressure.
3. **Role-Balanced Team Structure for Batters**:
   T20 cricket requires specialists across distinct phases. The Top 10 Batters list is constructed to balance:
   - **3 Top-Order Anchors** (Batting positions 1–3)
   - **4 Middle-Order Enforcers** (Batting positions 4–5)
   - **3 Lower-Order Finishers** (Batting positions 6+)

1. **The Phenomenon of Shashank Singh & Dinesh Karthik**:

2. **The Unbreachable Economy of Narine and Bumrah**:
   Under high pressure ($PI \ge 65$), Sunil Narine actually *improved* his economy from 7.02 to **6.48**, and Jasprit Bumrah conceded just **7.36 runs per over** while delivering over 700 balls in crunch moments—accumulating **+276.4 Runs Saved Above Expected (RSAE)**.
3. **Arshdeep Singh's Sheer Crunch Volume**:
   Arshdeep Singh bowled **1,354 pressure deliveries** and took **108 wickets**—the highest volume in the entire dataset across 2022–2026.

## 7. Model Reliability & Limitations

1. **Impact Player Rule Distortions (2023–2026)**:
   The introduction of the Impact Player in IPL 2023 lengthened batting lineups, naturally raising average death-over strike rates by ~7%. While our situational expectancy model adjusts for yearly trends, players competing primarily in 2024–2026 faced slightly higher baseline scoring environments.
2. **Pitch and Venue Confounders**:
   Certain high-altitude or flat grounds (e.g., M. Chinnaswamy, Rajiv Gandhi Stadium) experience inflated scoring compared to spinning surfaces (e.g., Chepauk, Ekana). Future iterations of SupeRR-ClutchML can integrate pitch-soil and boundary-dimension tracking.
3. **Cross-Match Consistency Verification**:
   The consistency penalty ensured that players who had a single outlier match did not surpass reliable performers who sustained excellence across 15+ match crucibles.
